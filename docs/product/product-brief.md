# HUSH product brief

**Status:** Draft, waiting for approval from Sarah and Lucas.

## Pitch

HUSH helps couples share the infrequent, invisible household work that nobody remembers until it is overdue, so neither person has to be the one who keeps it all in their head.

HUSH stands for "Handling Unseen Stuff at Home". The cheeky version is "Helping Us Stop Hollering".

## Problem

Some household work happens only a few times a year, so it never becomes a habit: grooming the dog, changing the HVAC filter, servicing the car, renewing a passport or registration, buying a gift in time, and preparing the house for each season. Nobody writes these down, and usually one person ends up carrying them in their head. When one is missed, it costs money or causes an argument.

## Audience

Couples who share a home. The first two users are Lucas and Sarah. Other couples may use it later, but it is built for the two of us first.

## Core value

- One shared place for the work that is easy to forget.
- Reminders that arrive early enough to act on, with a due date that comes from the real situation (breed and activity, mileage, lead time) and not a guess.
- Clear ownership, so a task is assigned to one person or visible to both.
- Reminders that never feel naggy.

## Non-goals

- Not a daily chore app. HUSH is for infrequent work, not dishes or laundry.
- Not monetized. It is a personal project and a backend portfolio piece.
- Not built for scale. It runs on free services (see `docs/cost-plan.md`).

## Success criteria

- Both Lucas and Sarah use HUSH every week for a month.
- Sarah reports fewer forgotten tasks over that month.
- Nothing in the app is described as "naggy" by either of us at a sprint review.
